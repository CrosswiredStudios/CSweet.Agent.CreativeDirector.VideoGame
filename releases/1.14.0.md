# 1.14.0

- Project reviews no longer report a failed reconciliation as completed. The review card now names the
  project's next step and who owns it, records consecutive failures of that step in operating state,
  and retries every ten minutes.
- After three consecutive failures of the same next step, the Director sends the CEO one direct message
  naming the step and the last error. A new stall can be reported again after the step succeeds.
- A Producer kickoff whose documentation-handoff task cannot be queued no longer fails the chat turn.
  The Producer is told what happens next and that the review retries automatically.
- Producer pitch-brief reviews accept provider responses that wrap the JSON in a code fence or append
  reasoning markup, matching the Producer's parser.
- No new capabilities or grants.
