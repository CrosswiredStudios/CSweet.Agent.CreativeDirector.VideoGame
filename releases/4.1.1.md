Uses the optional `CSweet.Agent.SDK.VideoGame.Contracts` 1.0.0 NuGet package for the shared video-game contracts instead of compiling its own `extensions/video-game/VideoGameContracts.cs` copy. Consumers still on extension 1.x keep their own `VideoGameContracts.cs`; their own provenance checks are unaffected, but `-VerifyOnly` across all consumers reports them until they move to the package.

Also carries `GameProposedWorkItemV1.BrowserVerification` and `GameBrowserVerification` through the package. No behavior change.
