# 1.14.2

- Delivery blockers from my reports now go up the reporting chain. When a report (for example the Producer)
  raises a stopped ticket it can't clear (`Retry ticket` / `Amend ticket` / `Replan ticket` decision requests),
  I forward it once, attributed, to my manager and tell the report it's been passed on. Previously I answered that
  the message was outside creative direction (or lacked a project context), so the blocker went no further and the
  sprint stalled. Forwarding takes no action and grants nothing; the decider replies to the report directly.
- No new capabilities or grants.
