# Creative pillars

## 1. Synaesthetic Feedback

Every meaningful gameplay event must be perceivable as both musical and visual information.

# Player experience

## Core fantasy

The player is a resonant conductor in a dying crystalline world. Each launch, bounce, brick hit, and chain rebuilds the lost song of the world.

# Gameplay

## Approved core mechanic

The player controls a paddle, launches a ball, and breaks geometric bricks. Every ball-brick interaction produces rhythmically synchronized audio. The level’s musical track is composed through gameplay.

# Presentation

## Art direction
